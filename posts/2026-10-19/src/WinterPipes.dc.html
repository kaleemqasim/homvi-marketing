HOME TIP · WINTER PIPES

Find the water shut-off before you need it.

1
Find and label the main shut-off
Make sure everyone at home knows where it is
2
Insulate exposed pipes
Garage, attic, crawl space and other unheated spots
3
Seal gaps near pipes
Where cold air sneaks in from outside
4
Away on a trip? Keep the heat on
A low setting, not switched off
5
Very cold night? Let a faucet drip
And open sink cabinets on outside walls
